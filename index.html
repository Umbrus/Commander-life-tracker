How Commander Pod Works

1

Start or Scan Into a Pod

Choose your starting life (20, 30, or 40) and tap Create New Pod to generate a 4-letter code and scannable QR invite. Everyone else scans to join—no accounts required.

2

Track on Your Own Phone

Tap the left or right half of your life card for −1 / +1, or press & hold for −5 / +5. Adding Commander Damage automatically deducts from your life total and syncs to every screen in real time.

3

Host Handoff & Table Tools

Tap any opponent's card to inspect their stats or transfer Pod Ownership if you need to leave early. Open the ☰ menu for Partner Commanders, extra counters, dice, and the life log.

Pro Tip: Add this page to your phone's Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen or Chrome Install App) for full-screen play without browser bars.
