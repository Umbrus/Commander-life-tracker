How CommandSync Works

1

Start or Scan Into a Pod

Choose 20-life Standard, 40-life Commander, or a custom starting total on the Play tab, then tap Host New Pod to select your deck and generate a pod code & QR invite.

2

Track Life, Eliminations & Turns

Tap left/right for −1 / +1, or press & hold to accelerate through ±5 and ±10. Reaching 0 life, 10 poison, or 21 Commander damage automatically marks a player knocked out and skips their turn.

3

Deck Locker & Match Analytics

Use the Decks tab to import Archidekt/Moxfield lists and customize Secret Lair art, and check Stats to view monthly, quarterly, and yearly win rates.
