How CommandSync Works

1

Start or Scan Into a Pod

Choose your starting life (20, 30, or 40) and tap Create New Pod to generate a 4-letter code and scannable QR invite. Everyone else scans to join—no accounts required.

2

Track Life, Eliminations & Turns

Tap the left or right half of your life card for −1 / +1, or press & hold for −5 / +5. Reaching 0 life, 10 poison, or 21 Commander damage automatically marks a player knocked out and skips their turn.

3

Deck Locker & Win/Loss Stats

Open the top-right ☰ menu to save your Moxfield or Archidekt decks, view full 100-card decklists, and automatically log wins and losses when you pilot a deck in a pod.
