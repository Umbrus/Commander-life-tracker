How CommandSync Works

1

Start or Scan Into a Pod

Choose your starting life (20, 30, or 40) on the Play tab and tap Create New Pod to generate a 4-letter code and scannable QR invite.

2

Track Life, Eliminations & Turns

Tap left/right for −1 / +1, or press & hold for −5 / +5. Reaching 0 life, 10 poison, or 21 Commander damage automatically marks a player knocked out and skips their turn.

3

Deck Locker & Match Analytics

Use the Decks tab to import Archidekt/Moxfield lists and customize Secret Lair art, and check Stats to view monthly, quarterly, and yearly win rates.
